# James’ Notes from Discipleship

The Ministration of Death (2Co 3:7)
is the law of sin & death (curses), called the Ministry of Condemnation in the same chapter.

It's Sacrificial Law, NOT the
Ten Commandments/the Holy Covenant which comes with the (blessings) of keeping the covenant given at Horeb.

The Ministration of Righteousness mentioned in 2Co 3 are the (Blessings) of the Everlasting Holy Covenant given at Horeb: Ten Commandments.

Messiah says we must keep the 10C to enter life in Mat 19:17 & Rev 22:14.

2 cor 3:7 is referencing both the blessings of keeping the Holy Covenant & curses which was
re-written with plaster on stone in Joshua 8, not engraved with the finger of YAHOVAH.

2Cor 3:7 Paul is saying that the Israelites who considered the ministration of death written in plaster in Joshua's time to be glorious in that it saved them & their whole race from being exterminated by God, how much more, the Glory of the Spirit of the Grace of God which brings eternal salvation...

The glory of Yeshua surpasses the glory of the law, but that doesn’t mean that the law is “nullified” as Christians wrongly claim, neither does it mean "all" of the 613 remain as over-zealous Torah keepers say.
