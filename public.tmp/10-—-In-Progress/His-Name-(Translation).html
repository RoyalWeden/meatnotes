His Name (Translation)

31 Mar 2026 · updated 5d ago · 4 min read

  • God’s Name from Strong’s Concordance

Jesus is the name of Baal, the Antichrist.

John 5:43 I am come in my Father’s name, and ye receive me not: if another shall come in his own name, him ye will receive.

Acts 4:12 Neither is there salvation in any other: for there is none other name under heaven given among men, whereby we must be saved.

God’s name was replaced in the Bible 6,568 (6,519 + 49) times to LORD.

God’s Name from Strong’s Concordance

Hebrew is written right to left

H3068, 6,159x

יְהֹוָה Yᵉhôvâh, yeh-ho-vaw’; from H1961; (the) self-Existent or Eternal; Jeho-vah, Jewish national name of God:—Jehovah, the Lord. Compare H3050, H3069.

H3050, 49x

יָהּ Yâhh, yaw; contraction for H3068, and meaning the same; Jah, the sacred name:—Jah, the Lord, most vehement. Compare names in ‘-iah,’ ‘-jah.’

Forms of God’s name:

  1. Yah
  2. Yehovah
  3. Jehovah

Prefixes used for God’s name:

  1. ‘Yeho-’
  2. ‘Jeho-’

Suffixes used for God’s name:

  1. ‘-yah’
  2. ‘-iah’
  3. ‘-jah’

H3091, 218x

יְהוֹשׁוּעַ Yᵉhôwshûwaʻ, yeh-ho-shoo’-ah; or יְהוֹשֻׁעַ Yᵉhôwshuʻa; from H3068 and H3467; Jehovah-saved; Jehoshua (i.e. Joshua), the Jewish leader:—Jehoshua, Jehoshuah, Joshua. Compare H1954, H3442.

Greek Septuagint → The Septuagint, the first known translation of the Bible into Greek, was written between the 3rd and 1st centuries BCE.

Latin Vulgate → The Vulgate is a late-4th-century Latin translation of the Bible.

The Vulgate is usually credited as being the first translation of the Old Testament into Latin directly from the Hebrew Tanakh rather than from the Greek Septuagint.

Names Must Sound the Same/Similar in Transliteration

Jacob: H3290 → G2384

  • יַעֲקֹב Yaʻăqôb → ΙΑΚΩΒ (Ιακώβ)
  • Greek pronunciation: ΙΑ-ΚΩΒ → ya-kobe
  • Latin, Genesis 25:27 (VUL) → Iacob (ia-cob)

Daniel: H1840 → G1158

  • דִּנִיֵּאל Dânîyêʼl → ΔΑΝΙΗΛ (Δανιήλ)
  • Greek pronunciation: ΔΑΝ-Ι-ΗΛ → dan-ee-ale’
  • Hebrew contains ‘God’ at end: אל (el)
  • Latin, Daniel 1:6 (VUL) → Daniel (dan-i-el)

Samuel: H8050 → G4545

  • שְׁמוּאֵל Shᵉmûwʼêl → ΣΑΜΟΥΗΛ (Σαμουήλ)
  • Greek pronunciation: ΣΑΜ-ΟΥ-ΗΛ → sam-oo-ale’
    • ⭐ ‘Σ’ makes the ‘s’ sound, replaces ‘sh’ sound
    • ⭐ ‘ΟΥ’ makes the ‘oo’/‘u’ sound
  • Latin, 1 Samuel 1:20 → Samuhel (sam-u-hel)
    • ⭐ ‘s’ replaces ‘sh’ sound
    • ⭐ ‘u’ replaces ‘ûw’ sound

  • יְהוֹשָׁפָט Yᵉhôwshâphâṭ → ΙΩΣΑΦΑΤ (Iωσαφάτ)
  • Hebrew contains Yehovah’s name prefix: יְה (yeho)
  • Name meaning: ‘Yehovah has judged’
  • Greek pronunciation: ΙΩ-ΣΑ-ΦΑΤ → yo-suh-phat
    • ⭐ ‘Yeho-’ becomes ‘ΙΩ-’ (yo)
  • ‘Josaphat’ is from Greek spelling
    • ⭐ ‘ΙΩ-’ becomes ‘Jo-’
  • Latin, 2 Samuel 8:16 → Iosaphat (io-sa-phat)
    • ⭐ ‘Yeho-’ becomes ‘Io-‘
