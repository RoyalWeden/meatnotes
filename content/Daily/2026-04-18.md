# Root for Zion is “chief musician”

> Zion ([H6726](https://www.blueletterbible.org/lexicon/h6726/kjv/wlc/0-1/))
>
> צִיּוֹן Tsîyôwn, tsee-yone'; the same (regularly) as [H6725](https://www.blueletterbible.org/lexicon/H6725/kjv/wlc/); Tsijon (as a permanent capital), a mountain of Jerusalem:—Zion.

↓

> [H6725](https://www.blueletterbible.org/lexicon/H6725/kjv/wlc/)
>
> צִיּוּן tsîyûwn, tsee-yoon'; from the same as [H6723](https://www.blueletterbible.org/lexicon/H6723/kjv/wlc/) in the sense of conspicuousness (compare [H5329](https://www.blueletterbible.org/lexicon/H5329/kjv/wlc/)); a monumental or guiding pillar:—sign, title, waymark.

↓

> [H5329](https://www.blueletterbible.org/lexicon/H5329/kjv/wlc/)
>
> נָצַח nâtsach, naw-tsakh'; a primitive root; also as denominative from [H5331](https://www.blueletterbible.org/lexicon/H5331/kjv/wlc/) properly, to glitter from afar, i.e. to be eminent (as a superintendent, especially of the Temple services and its music); to be permanent:—excel, ***~chief musician (singer)~***, oversee(-r), set forward.
