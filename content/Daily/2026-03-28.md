## Urim and Thummim

> Leviticus 8:8 And he put the breastplate upon him: also he put in the breastplate the Urim and the Thummim.

urim → light

> urim ([H224](https://www.blueletterbible.org/lexicon/h224/kjv/wlc/0-1/))
>
> אוּרִים ʼÛwrîym, oo-reem'; plural of [H217](https://www.blueletterbible.org/lexicon/H217/kjv/wlc/); ***~lights~***; Urim, the oracular brilliancy of the figures in the high-priest's breastplate:—Urim.

light → judgment

> thummim ([H8550](https://www.blueletterbible.org/lexicon/h8550/kjv/wlc/0-1/))
>
> תֻּמִּים Tummîym, toom-meem'; plural of [H8537](https://www.blueletterbible.org/lexicon/H8537/kjv/wlc/); ***~perfections~***, i.e. (techn.) one of the epithets of the objects in the high-priest's breastplate as an emblem of complete Truth:—Thummim.

urim thummin → perfect judgment or yeshua doing righteous judgment
