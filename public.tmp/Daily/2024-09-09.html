trinity debunk ⭐

🔹 The Father is the supreme deity, who sent Messiah His Son. (John 17:3)

📚**John 17:3 - King James Version with Apocrypha (KJVA)**

And this is life eternal, that they might know thee the only true God, and Jesus Christ, whom thou hast sent.

💡Yah, rendered as God in the King James is Strong’s Greek Dictionary word 2316 theos, which means: a deity, especially (with 3588) the supreme Divinity; figuratively, a magistrate; by Hebraism, very:—X exceeding, God, god(-ly, -ward).

[Long before the NT was written, 2316 (theós) referred to the supreme being who owns and sustains all things.]

John 20:17; Rev 3:12 (called Him God, four times)

🎺🔹 1 Timothy 2:5 declares that there is one God, the Father; and one Mediator between Him and men. 💡 That makes no sense if Messiah is the same God as the Father.

📚**1 Timothy 2:5 - King James Version with Apocrypha (KJVA)**

👉For there is one God,👈 and 👉one mediator between God and men,👈 the man Christ Jesus;

🎺🔹 Mark 12:29 gives us an example of how the King James muddies the waters with the titles of God and Lord, “And Jesus answered him, The first of all the commandments is, Hear, O Israel; The Lord our God is one Lord:”

It should be:

“And Yeshua answered him, “The first of all the commands is, ‘Hear, O Israel, YHUH our God, YHUH is one.”

Using the proper names, we see who is God, YHUH. It’s pointing to the Father, not Messiah.
